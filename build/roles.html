Module roles¶

Module containing classes for creating game roles.

Game Roles: 1. Protagonist - The main character, a student at a programming school. 2. NPC - Characters that can be interacted with (Peers). 3. Enemy (Projects, Verter) - Enemies that must be fought (projects and their verification system).

The protagonist interacts with NPCs, completes projects, and their knowledge and skills grow depending on the success of the projects.
